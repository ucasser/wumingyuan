State Construction in the Twentieth Century
——— the Perspective of War and Revolution
Abstract: There is deep connection between war，revolution and the construction of nation －state．In
the 20th Century，China experienced two competitions about the ways of national state construction:
the first is between revolutionaries and constitutionalists at the beginning of the 20th Century，revolu-
tionaries，whose leader was Sun Yat －sen who established the Republic of China by democratic revo-
lution．However，they had never been unable to establish a strong nation．The second is the competi-
tion between Communist Party of China and Nationalist Party in the middle of the 20th Century．At
last，CPC established a strong national state through the way of class nationalism．War and revolution
are the main threads in these two competitions，but revolutionary thinking continued in quite a long
period after the PRC，and brought about heavy calamities．China has a“glorious tradition of revolu-
tionary”，and it is a question we need to think about that how to handle the tradition．China has en-
tered the era of comprehensive deepening reformation．The reformation is another kind of revolution．
Maybe only after continuous and steady reformation can social organism get healthy and stable devel-
opment．In this sense，the reformation is an on －going process that must never stop
